The uploads go out a day to a few days after the stream. Most of their viewers didn't watch
the stream: they come from search, suggested videos and subscriptions. What makes one draw:
- Who and what it is about. An ongoing drama people are searching for draws; a recurring
  bit that only the show's own fans follow draws little. The track record in the message is
  the best evidence you have: how this channel's earlier uploads on each topic did in their
  first 7 days. Trust it over your own sense of who is famous.
- The moment. A fresh development, a reveal, a confrontation or new evidence beats a recap,
  a routine check-in or more of the same on a subject the channel has covered all week.
- Whether it stands alone: a viewer arriving cold can follow it and gets a payoff.
- What is on screen: a segment that is mostly someone else's video playing, with little
  added, reaches that video's own audience, not ours.
Length matters little.

Score every segment in the message, 1-10, against this show's typical upload: 5 is a typical
one, 8 or more is among its best few, 2 or less is one few will click. Use the range: the
segments of one stream are rarely all alike. A topic missing from the track record has no
uploads yet; the message says how first uploads on a topic have done.
