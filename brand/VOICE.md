# Clippa voice

Night Watch voice guide for review tools, the war room, and Discord cards.

## Voice

Clippa speaks like a calm watch officer at mission control for clips: confident, brief, and factual. It reports what is on the board and what happens next, in the present tense, without hype or alarm. The human stays in charge — approve, hold, or re-split — and silence in the grace window is a decision, not a takeover. Copy should sound like a handoff, not an alert.

## No “we”

Never use *we*, *us*, or *our*. Clippa is a tool, not a team. Refer to Clippa by name, write in the third person, or leave the subject out. Clippa speaks as itself or impersonally.

Do: Clippa stays up. You stay in charge.

Don’t: We stay up. You stay in charge.

## Principles

| Principle | Do | Don’t |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No we. Clippa is a tool, not a team. | Clippa stays up. You stay in charge. | We stay up. You stay in charge. |
| State the fact, then the next action. | Held. Two findings on Cut 04. | We found some issues you may want to look at! |
| Keep the human in charge. | Auto-approves in 02:00:00 unless you hold it. | All set — we published this for you. |
| Name the thing plainly. | Finding: subject name does not match the source label. | Our AI caught a potential problem. |
| Stay level. No hype, no alarm. | Cut 06 is over 50 minutes. Flagged for re-split. | This segment is way too long. Act now! |

The voice stays spare in every state. What changes is how direct the next action is, not the volume.

## Word list

### Preferred terms

| Prefer | Use it for | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| hold | Pause a cut so it does not auto-approve | block, reject, quarantine |
| finding | A review-check result that needs a look | issue, violation, error (reserve error for a system failure) |
| clear | No findings, or a hold that has been released | greenlit, unblocked |
| cut | The item under review, as in Cut 04 | asset, piece, content |
| re-split | Flag a cut, such as a segment over 50 minutes, to be split again | recut, chop |
| on watch | The stream is being watched | monitoring, live AI surveillance |

### Words to avoid

| Avoid | Why |
| --- | --- |
| we, us, our | Clippa is a tool, not a team. Name Clippa, write in the third person, or leave the subject out. |
| AI magic, seamlessly, effortlessly, revolutionary, game-changing | Hype. Clippa does not sell a trick. |
| our AI decided, auto-magic, just sit back | Takes the decision away from the human. |
| killer, crush, nail, juice | Marketing slang. It does not belong on a review board. |
| Stacked exclamation points, or emoji as the only status | Alarm and decoration are not a handoff. |

### Style

- **Capitalization.** Sentence case for sentences and status lines. The product name is Clippa; the wordmark is lowercase `clippa`. Kind names are Short, Clip, and Segment. Status words are Held, Approved, Auto-approved, Finding, Re-split, On watch, and Pending. Never all caps for emphasis.
- **Timecodes.** `HH:MM:SS` with leading zeros. Ranges use an en dash and no spaces: `02:14:08–02:15:41`. A grace window of 120 minutes is `02:00:00`. Remaining time under one hour may be `MM:SS` (`11:40`).
- **Numbers.** Use numerals: 3 findings, 1 hold, 50 minutes, score 7.8, cost $4.12. Do not spell out 120.

## Microcopy

Each moment has two forms. The Discord card line is a short sentence that can stand alone on a card. The war-room status line is denser, for the board. Figures are examples of the existing review flow, not a live stream or a billing product.

| Moment | Discord card line | War-room status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Short held because of findings | Cut 04 held. 1 finding on this Short. | Cut 04 · Held · 1 finding |
| Short held for other voices | Cut 08 held for other voices. It stays off publish until someone releases it. | Cut 08 · Held · other voices |
| Vision or text finding | Finding on Cut 04: subject name does not match the source label. | Cut 04 · Finding · subject name does not match the source label |
| Auto-approve countdown at 120 minutes | No objection yet. Cut 05 auto-approves in 02:00:00 unless held. | Cut 05 · Auto-approve 02:00:00 |
| Auto-approve countdown, close | Cut 05 auto-approves in 00:11:40 unless held. | Cut 05 · Auto-approve 11:40 |
| Auto-approved | Cut 05 auto-approved. No objection in the grace window. | Cut 05 · Auto-approved |
| Approved by a human | Cut 03 approved. | Cut 03 · Approved |
| Segment flagged for re-split, over 50 minutes | Cut 06 is over 50 minutes. Flagged for re-split. | Cut 06 · Re-split · over 50 min |
| Stream started processing | Stream A started processing. | Stream A · Processing started |
| Stream finished processing | Stream A finished processing. 7 cuts in review. | Stream A · Processing finished · 7 in review |
| Cost warning | Stream A cost is $4.12 today. | Stream A · Cost $4.12 |
| Error | Review check did not finish on Cut 07. Try again. | Cut 07 · Error · review check did not finish |
